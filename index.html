Lab tasks · VLAN 10 · 192.168.10.0/24
  • Create VLAN 10 named IT on SW1
  • Put SW1 gi1/0/1 and gi1/0/24 in access VLAN 10
  • Set R1 gi0/0 to 192.168.10.1 255.255.255.0 and bring it up
  • From PC-IT: ping 192.168.10.1
